How to play
  • Each player enters at the middle of their own side — shown by the colored ring on the board.
  • 1, 2, or 3 shells up → move that many spaces.
  • 4 up → move 4 spaces, then throw again.
  • 0 up → move 8 spaces, then throw again.
  • Land exactly on an opponent to send it back to hand — and throw again.
  • Keep throwing while you roll 4s and 0s. Banked throws are spent one at a time, on any of your pieces, in any order.
  • You must use every banked throw that has a legal move; ones that don't are skipped.
  • The four starting squares (diamond-marked) are safe from capture — for everyone.
  • Once any of your pieces captures, all of your pieces are permanently free to enter the inner rings.
  • Your pieces never block each other — they share squares and move independently.
  • You need the exact number remaining to land on the center. Overshoot and that piece can't move.
  • When the first player gets home you choose whether to play on for the remaining places.
